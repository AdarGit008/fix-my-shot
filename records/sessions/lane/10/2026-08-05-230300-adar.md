**The find of the lane:** the stock projection converges into
authority-rejected penetration on `follow-through-arm-collapsed-early-*` — ANY
small hand drag swings the hanging guide hand into the parked ball (hand_left↔ball,
10.2 mm) and the final three-check kills it, so both poses were uneditable
(lane/9's record had flagged "self-collision pairs beyond drag↔floor not wired").
Diagnosed by reconstructing the default formulation step-by-step: the QP converges
fine (vmax < 1e-6 @ step 21); it is the authority that says no. Fix:
`EDITOR_COLLISION_PAIRS` (ball × torso column + both arms; arms × head/torso; 20
pairs through the ported `CollisionAvoidanceLimit`, parent-child auto-filtered)
wired into every editor projection. Regression net now in the suite: **every**
shipped library pose must accept an in-phase hand nudge under the full editor
limit set, plus a load-bearing pair test (without collision rows → rejected;
with → accepted).

**Edit lifecycle** (`session.ts`, DOM-free): load (pins phase; envelope-checked,
fail-loud on library/bounds drift) → beginDrag (anchor) → previewDrag (2-step
incremental projection per pointer frame — ADR-0010) → endDrag: ONE authoritative
anchor→target projection (50 steps), belt-and-braces envelope re-check, then
commit + undo history, or reject-and-revert carrying a diagnosis of the previewed
pose (which gate check failed / which parameters left the envelope). Scripted-gate
unit tests for the whole lifecycle + a real-gate end-to-end (drag stance-clean,
commit, undo restores byte-for-byte).

**ADR-0010** (supersedes ADR-0009's CCDIKSolver-preview clause): the drag preview
is the gate's own incremental projection. Measured on this scene: 2-step preview
avg 0.98 ms · p95 1.73 ms per pointer frame — 5–10% of a 60 fps frame. A CCDIK
preview would mean a second kinematic model (bone mirror) + numeric decomposition
onto non-orthogonal multi-hinge axes (shoulder (2,1,1)/(0,−1,1)), drift-prone and
re-checked by the gate anyway. Preview is constraint-aware as a bonus: limbs stop
at phase bounds mid-drag, the ball tows with the hand.
